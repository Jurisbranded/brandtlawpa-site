Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Brandt Law, P.A. (“Brandt Law,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit brandtlawpa.com or contact us through it. We keep one privacy policy for everyone who uses this site, and we honor the rights that the law gives you wherever you live.

Who Is Responsible for Your Information

This website is operated by Brandt Law, P.A., a Florida law firm with its office in Lithia, Florida, that represents condominium and homeowners associations. Brandt Law is the party responsible for personal information collected through this website. For any privacy question or request, use the request forms in our Privacy Center, or email us.

Information We Collect

Information you give us

When you request an appointment, send us a privacy request, an unsubscribe request, or a DMCA notice through this site, or email us, we collect the details you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, telephone number, and the contents of your message. You decide what to send us; please do not send confidential information about a legal matter through this site.

Forms on this site are sent to us through our own form service and are checked by Cloudflare Turnstile, a security check that screens out automated spam.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, this site’s servers and cookies may automatically record limited technical information when you visit, for example your IP address, browser type, device and operating system, the pages you view, and the date and time of your visit. This information helps us keep the site secure, diagnose problems, and understand how the site is used. On this site that means Cloudflare, which hosts this site and protects it from attacks and spam, and Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without using cookies; and Google Analytics, which helps us understand in aggregate how the site is used (it loads only after you scroll, tap or press a key, or a few seconds after a page loads, and you can turn it off on our Opt-Out Preferences page). Our Cookie Policy explains the cookies and similar technologies we use and how you can control them.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • respond to your inquiries and process the requests you submit;
  • provide, maintain, secure, and improve this website;
  • send you communications you have asked to receive, and let you unsubscribe at any time;
  • comply with our legal and professional obligations; and
  • establish, exercise, or defend legal claims where necessary.

We do not use the information you submit through this site to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose information only in these limited circumstances: to trusted service providers who host, maintain, or support this website and our communications (such as Cloudflare, Google, and our own email and form services), under obligations of confidentiality and only as needed to perform their services; when required by law, subpoena, court order, or governmental request; and to protect the rights, property, or safety of Brandt Law, our clients, or others. If Brandt Law is ever involved in a merger, acquisition, or similar transaction, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

No Attorney-Client Relationship

Reading this site, or contacting us through it, does not create an attorney-client relationship, and information you send us through the site is not treated as confidential unless we already represent you. See our Disclaimer.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to receive a copy in a portable form, to opt out of marketing, to opt out of any “sale” or “sharing” of personal information, and to be free from discrimination for exercising these rights. Residents of jurisdictions such as the European Union, the United Kingdom, California and other U.S. states, Canada, Brazil, and elsewhere are afforded these rights under their applicable laws, and we extend the same core controls to everyone.

To exercise any of these rights, use the forms in our Privacy Center or email us. We will verify your request, respond within the time your law requires, and will not charge you for a first reasonable request. Some information may have to be kept to meet our legal and professional record-keeping obligations; if so, we will tell you. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, to respond to and document your requests, and to meet our legal, tax, and record-keeping obligations. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

How We Protect Information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information collected through this site. No website or method of transmission is completely secure, however, so please do not send confidential or time-sensitive information about a legal matter through this site’s forms or by email.

Cloudflare Turnstile (Spam and Bot Protection)

Forms on this website are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, a security service from Cloudflare, Inc. Turnstile may run in invisible mode: it checks in the background, with nothing shown on screen, that a form is being sent by a person and not by an automated program. To do this it processes limited technical information from your browser and device, such as your IP address and browser characteristics, and uses it only to protect this website from spam and abuse.

Cloudflare’s processing of this information is described in the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum, which applies together with the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

International Visitors

Brandt Law and this website operate in the United States. If you access the site from outside the United States, understand that your information may be processed in the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your location. Where the law requires, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.
