Accessibility Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Brandt Law, P.A. wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use assistive technology such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, voice control, or a keyboard instead of a mouse.

Our Standard

We build and maintain this website to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium. In practice that means:

  • every page has a single main heading and headings in a logical order;
  • images that carry meaning have text descriptions, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers;
  • text and controls have strong color contrast, and the page can be zoomed without losing content;
  • every link, button, menu and form works from the keyboard, with a visible focus outline;
  • every form field has a written label, required fields are marked in words, and form messages are announced to screen readers;
  • videos from other sites load only when you choose to play them.

Accessibility Tools

The site also offers the UserWay accessibility tool (the accessibility icon on each page). It lets you change text size and spacing, switch to a dyslexia-friendly font, increase contrast, pause animations and more. The site is built to be accessible without it; the tool is an extra.

Content From Others

Some pages link to websites run by others, such as government sites and statutes. We do not control those sites and cannot guarantee their accessibility.
