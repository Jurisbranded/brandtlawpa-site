Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Brandt Law, P.A. uses cookies and similar technologies on brandtlawpa.com, and how you can control them. It applies alongside our Privacy Policy.

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device to make the site work, to remember your preferences, and to understand how the site is used. Similar technologies, such as local storage, pixels, and tags, do comparable things. We group everything this site uses into the categories below.

The Categories We Use

Strictly necessary. These cookies are required for the site to function: page navigation, security, and remembering your choices (for example, if you turn off Google Analytics). They are always on and cannot be switched off, because the site cannot work properly without them. On this site they include the security cookies Cloudflare sets to protect the site and its forms from automated abuse.

Statistics. These help us understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site: which pages are viewed and how people move through them, so we can improve it. This site uses Google Analytics for this; it sets its own cookies (their names start with _ga), and we do not use them to identify you personally. It loads only after you scroll, tap or press a key, or a few seconds after a page loads, and you can turn it off at any time on our Opt-Out Preferences page. Cloudflare Web Analytics also counts visits, without cookies.

Marketing. This site does not run third-party advertising networks and sets no marketing cookies.

Your Consent and Controls

This site does not show a cookie banner, because it sets no marketing cookies. You can turn off Google Analytics at any time through Opt-Out Preferences in our Privacy Center. Turning it off will not prevent you from reading the site.

You can also control cookies through your browser, blocking or deleting them, or browsing privately. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or “Do Not Track” signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of any non-essential cookies (Google Analytics does not load) and of any “sale” or “sharing” of your information.

Cookies Set by Others

The accessibility tool on this site (UserWay) may remember the display settings you choose in your browser. If a page includes a YouTube video, it loads only when you press play, and YouTube may then set its own cookies under its own policy. We limit such embeds and disclose them where used.

Changes to This Policy

If we add or change the technologies this site uses, we will update this policy and revise the “Last updated” date above.

Contact Us

Questions about cookies can be sent to our privacy team (email us), or submitted through the Privacy Center.
